# Partner 360° View

## Purpose
Comprehensive view of everything related to one partner — deals, offers, invoices, documents, KYC, portal access, commissions, activities, notes — in one place.

## Access
- Partners list → click a partner row → "360° View" button in detail sheet

## Sections
1. **Header** — name, type, status, risk score, KYC status, portal tier
2. **Contact & Address** — email, phone, address, contact person
3. **Trade Preferences** — currency, payment terms, incoterm
4. **Bank Details** — bank name, account, IBAN, SWIFT
5. **Deals** — all deals with this partner (status, value, stage)
6. **Offers** — all offers sent to this partner
7. **Invoices** — all invoices for this partner
8. **Documents** — shared documents (contracts, specs, etc.)
9. **KYC** — submission status, reviewer, review date, documents
10. **Portal Access** — tier, status, last login, email
11. **Commissions** — if partner is a commission agent, their commissions
12. **Activities** — historical activity log (KYC approved, invoices issued, etc.)
13. **Notes** — internal notes about this partner

## Tips
- Use this view before calling a client — everything you need is here
- The Activities section pulls from the old system's activity log + new audit log
- Notes are internal — clients cannot see them
